## 1. Disclosure Statement (as included in Paper Acknowledgments)

> *Generative AI tools (Google Antigravity, including Anthropic Claude and Google Gemini models) were used for drafting and editing text, and for generating analysis scripts, tables, and figures. All AI-generated text and code were reviewed, verified, and edited by the authors, who take full responsibility for the content. Relevance labels were produced by human annotators without AI assistance. An LLM was also used to extract queries from job descriptions, as described in Section III.*
