Phase~1 expands the corpus to 100 repositories and adds Python and Go. 
Phase~2 refines the AST parser to reduce fallbacks below $10\%$ and repeats the comparison on pure AST chunks. 
Phase~3 (RQ3) ablates header components (path, class, signature). 
Phase~4 (RQ4) asks about 20 practitioners to verify requirement-evidence pairs with and without retrieved evidence and measures accuracy, time, and confidence. 
Citation-grounded generation is left to future work.

\subsection{Risks and Mitigation}
\textbf{Annotation Overhead:} Mitigated via pooling depth $k=3$ and dual annotation of newly pooled candidates. 
\textbf{License Governance:} In later phases, we will include only repositories whose licenses explicitly permit redistribution. 
\textbf{Ethics:} The study evaluates evidence retrieval for requirements; it does not score individual people, and any future study with human subjects will be reviewed and informed consent obtained. 
\textbf{LLM Reliability:} The query extractor and any LLM judge will be validated against human labels.
